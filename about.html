About the owner & author

Hi, I'm William David — I started ProLift to do this the right way

Every article, every service page, every piece of advice you read on this site comes from me personally — someone who has replaced thousands of springs, diagnosed hundreds of openers, and learned the hard lessons that only come from real fieldwork.

I founded ProLift Garage Doors in 2011 with a single goal: offer Connecticut homeowners a garage door company that doesn't upsell, doesn't inflate problems, and doesn't disappear after the job. Most of what I know I learned on driveways across Fairfield and Hartford counties — in the cold, under cars, on ladders — not in a training room.

Beyond running the service side, I write every word published under the ProLift name. When I explain why your torsion spring broke, or how to tell a bad gear from a bad logic board, that's knowledge I earned doing the work — not content I outsourced. I believe the homeowner who understands their garage door is the homeowner who doesn't get taken advantage of.

Today ProLift serves all of Connecticut, Monday through Saturday, with the same flat-rate pricing and commitment to quality on every job. Same tech who picks up the phone is the one showing up at your door.
