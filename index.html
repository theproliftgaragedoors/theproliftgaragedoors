Common questions

What CT homeowners ask us every day

Straight answers so you know exactly what to expect before you call ProLift.

If your opener is under 10–12 years old and the issue is a broken gear, logic board, or drive mechanism, repair usually wins. If it's older, grinding regularly, or failing repeatedly, replacement is often the smarter long-term call. Our technician gives an honest recommendation either way — no pressure toward the more expensive option.
Call before noon and we're typically there the same day. For urgent situations — opener stuck with a car inside, or a door that won't close — we prioritize immediately. Our typical arrival window is 2–4 hours from your call, 7 days a week.
There's a standard service call fee that covers the technician's time and diagnostic inspection. That fee is fully waived when you proceed with the repair. We always give a complete diagnosis and flat-rate quote first — you're never obligated to proceed.

Absolutely — off-track repair is one of our most frequent calls. Do not try to force the door open or closed; it causes significant extra damage. Call us immediately and we'll get it safely back on track, usually the same day.
Grinding usually means worn gears inside the opener motor, damaged rollers, or a misaligned track. None of these should be ignored — they worsen fast and can lead to complete failure. Our tech will diagnose the exact cause and fix it in one visit.
