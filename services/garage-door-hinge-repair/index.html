Signs You Need It
Signs You Need
Hinge Repair & Replacement

Catching these early usually means a smaller repair instead of an emergency call. If any of these sound familiar, call us — we'll tell you honestly whether it needs a visit.

Squeaking or cracking at the joints

Dry or worn hinges make themselves heard every cycle, and the noise gets worse as the metal fatigues.

Hinge visibly cracked or bent

A cracked hinge is a structural failure — the door section it holds is no longer properly supported.

Door sections sagging

If one section hangs lower than the rest, its hinges have stretched or the screws have pulled out of the panel.

Screws pulling out of the panel

Stripped screw holes mean the hinge can't hold — the fix is new hardware properly anchored, not bigger screws in the same hole.

Door noisy at the joints

Popping or clicking as each section passes the curve of the track usually means worn hinge knuckles.

Door looks uneven when open

Sections that don't fold evenly point to a hinge that is bent or has a seized knuckle.

What hinges do — and why they fail

A sectional garage door folds at the hinges every time it opens, and each hinge carries the weight of the sections below it. The bottom hinges carry the most load, and the top roller carriers take the most stress at the curve of the track. Over thousands of cycles the knuckles wear, the metal fatigues, and the screws work loose in the panel. Cheaper hinges — thin-gauge steel — crack sooner, especially on heavier insulated or wood doors. Connecticut's freeze-thaw cycles don't help: moisture gets into the knuckles, rust forms, and a hinge that was already worn seizes up.

The signs most homeowners miss

Hinge problems start quietly. A slight squeak that lubrication doesn't fix, a section that sits a fraction lower than its neighbours, a small crack at the bend of a center hinge — all easy to overlook until the hinge fails completely and the door sags or jams. During a tune-up we check every hinge, because catching a cracked hinge early is a quick replacement; catching it late can mean a bent panel or a door that won't close.

What the repair involves

Hinge replacement is straightforward but must be done with the door properly supported — the sections above the hinge being replaced are carrying weight. We replace damaged hinges with heavy-gauge units matched to the door, repair any stripped screw holes with proper anchors, lubricate all the hinges, and test the door through full cycles. If several hinges are worn, we'll recommend replacing the set — the ones that look fine are usually not far behind.
