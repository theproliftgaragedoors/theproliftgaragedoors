Signs You Need It
Signs Your Garage Door
Needs a Repair

Catching these early usually means a smaller repair instead of an emergency call. If any of these sound familiar, call us — we'll tell you honestly whether it needs a visit.

Door won't open or close

The most common call we get. Usually a spring, cable, sensor, or opener issue — the specific cause changes the fix and what's involved.

Loud noises — grinding, banging, popping

New noises mean something is wearing, loose, or about to fail. The sooner it's checked, the smaller the repair.

Door is crooked or uneven

One side higher than the other, or the door sitting at an angle, usually means a cable, spring, or track problem.

Opener problems of any kind

Humming, reversing, not responding, or working only sometimes — we diagnose the opener and the door together.

Door stuck open or closed

A stuck door is a security problem and a safety problem. We prioritize stuck doors and get a technician to you quickly.

Door reverses for no reason

The safety system is doing its job — something is telling the opener the path isn't clear. We find the cause.

Any garage door problem, one call

You don't need to know what's wrong with your door before you call us — that's our job. Our trucks carry springs, cables, rollers, sensors, and common opener parts, so most repairs are completed in a single visit. If the problem turns out to be something unusual, we'll explain it plainly and arrange whatever is needed — the door is always left safe and secure in the meantime.

What we check on every repair visit

A garage door is a system, and the symptom is rarely the whole story. A door that won't open might have a broken spring — or a spring that broke because the door was out of balance, which was caused by a stretched cable. An opener that reverses might have a sensor fault — or a door that's binding in a bent track. That's why our technicians inspect the full system on every visit: springs, cables, rollers, hinges, tracks, opener, and sensors. We fix the fault you called about, and we tell you about anything else we find — with an honest assessment of what needs attention now and what can wait.
