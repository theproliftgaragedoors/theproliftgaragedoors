h2 id=local-issuesLocal garage door problems in West Suffieldh2
h3Harsh winters and freeze-thaw cyclesh3
pInland Connecticut winters bring hard freezes, snow and ice. Cold stiffens grease and seals, and a bottom seal can freeze to the concrete overnight, making the opener strain until a gear or limit setting gives way. If your door sticks on a frosty morning, stop pressing the button, inspect the seal, then call us.p
h3Oversized and detached barn or workshop doorsh3
pRural properties often have wide or tall doors, heavy wood panels and long runs of track. These need correctly sized springs and heavy-duty openers; a standard kit can wear out quickly. We measure the actual opening and weight.p
h3Dust, pollen and farm debrish3
pFields and farm activity put dust, chaff and pollen into tracks and onto sensor lenses, causing nuisance reversals and rough travel. Clean tracks with a dry cloth and wipe sensor lenses regularly.p
h3Storm outages and rural powerh3
pNor'easters and summer thunderstorms can cut power for hours, and rural roads may be slow to restore. Every opener has a red emergency release cord. A battery-backup opener is a worthwhile upgrade.p
h3Road salt and moistureh3
pSalt tracked in from winter roads corrodes bottom brackets, panels and seals. Rinse the door base and floor edge periodically.p
