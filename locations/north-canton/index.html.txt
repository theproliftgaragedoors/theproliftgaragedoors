<h2 id="services">Garage door services we provide in North Canton</h2>
<h3>Garage door spring replacement</h3>
<p>Springs counterbalance a door that can weigh well over 100 pounds. A torsion spring is rated for a limited number of cycles, and when it breaks you often hear a loud bang. The door then feels extremely heavy or will not lift. We replace springs in matched pairs where appropriate, match them to the door's weight and height, and test balance afterward. Never attempt this yourself; the stored energy is dangerous.</p>
<h3>Cable repair and roller replacement</h3>
<p>Cables carry the load when the door moves. Frayed, rusty or snapped cables can leave one side hanging crooked, and worn rollers cause grinding and shaking. We replace cables in pairs and upgrade worn rollers to quieter, longer-lasting options when it makes sense.</p>
<h3>Off-track and bent-track repair</h3>
<p>A door that has jumped its track is unsafe to operate, and forcing it can ruin panels. We secure the door, straighten or replace damaged track, realign the system and run full test cycles.</p>

<h2 id="howto">How to check and care for your garage door (step by step)</h2>
<h3>How to test your garage door balance</h3>
<ol>
<li>Close the door fully, then pull the red emergency release cord to disconnect the opener.</li>
<li>Lift the door by hand to about halfway.</li>
<li>Let go. A balanced door stays put. If it drops or shoots up, the springs are worn or out of adjustment.</li>
<li>Reconnect the opener only after the door is closed, and call a technician for any imbalance. Do not touch springs or cables.</li>
</ol>
<h3>How to test the safety reversal system</h3>
<ol>
<li>Lay a roll of paper towels or a 2x4 flat in the door's path.</li>
<li>Press the button to close the door.</li>
<li>It should reverse on contact. If not, stop using the opener and call us.</li>
<li>Wave a broom handle through the sensor beam while closing; the door should reverse immediately.</li>
</ol>
<h3>How to lubricate your garage door</h3>
<ol>
<li>Wipe dirt from hinges, springs and roller stems.</li>
<li>Apply silicone or lithium-based garage-door lubricant to hinges, roller bearings and the spring coil.</li>
<li>Do not grease the tracks; clean them with a dry cloth.</li>
<li>Cycle the door a few times and wipe off excess.</li>
</ol>
<h3>How to open your door manually in a power outage</h3>
<ol>
<li>Close the door if possible and make sure no one is beneath it.</li>
<li>Pull the red cord down and back to release the trolley.</li>
<li>Lift carefully by the handle. If it feels very heavy, the spring may be broken: stop and call a professional.</li>
</ol>
