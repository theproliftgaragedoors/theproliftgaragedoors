Opener Repair & Installation

Motor failing, reversing unexpectedly, or not responding? We repair and install all opener types — chain, belt, screw, and direct drive — from all major brands.

Spring Replacement

A broken torsion or extension spring is the #1 reason a door won't open. We carry springs for all door sizes and replace them safely — never a DIY job.

Cable Repair

Snapped or frayed cables cause dangerous imbalances. We replace both cables together so wear stays even and the door operates safely.

Off-Track Repair

Don't force a door that jumped its tracks — you'll cause more damage. Call us immediately; we realign and test the full track system before leaving.

New Door Installation

Full replacement or a new-home install — we measure, source, and install garage doors in all styles and materials with professional finishing.

Tune-Up & Maintenance

Annual maintenance prevents most breakdowns. Our tune-up covers lubrication, hardware tightening, balance check, sensor testing, and a full system inspection.
