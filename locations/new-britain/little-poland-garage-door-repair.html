Little Poland service context

Start with the symptom, not the guess.

Garage door problems often overlap. A noisy door may need rollers, track alignment, balancing, or opener repair. We check the complete system before recommending work.

When the door is stuck open

Keep people and vehicles away from the opening. Do not pull, force, or repeatedly activate the opener. Call ProLift so the spring, cable, track, and opener can be checked safely.

When the opener hums or reverses

Check for a blocked photo-eye, then stop using the system if it continues. Sensor alignment, wiring, travel limits, gears, or the motor may be involved.

When the door is loud or uneven

Grinding, scraping, and a crooked lift can point to worn rollers, hinges, cables, tracks, or balance. Continued operation can turn a small repair into panel or opener damage.

When repair may be better than replacement

A repair can make sense when the door structure is sound and the failure is isolated. We explain the tradeoff between repair, partial replacement, and a new door.
