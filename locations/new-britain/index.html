New Britain garage door FAQ

Questions homeowners ask.

Call if your situation is urgent or you are not sure whether the door is safe to use.

Can you provide same-day garage door repair in New Britain?

Appointment availability depends on the day and the repair area. Call before noon to ask about same-day options and tell us if the door is stuck open, off track, or trapping a vehicle.

Should I operate a garage door that came off its track?

No. Stop using it and keep people clear of the door. An off-track door can become heavier, fall, or damage panels and hardware.

How do I know if I need spring replacement?

A door that is suddenly heavy, will not lift, or has a visible gap in its spring may need spring service. Do not attempt spring replacement yourself; the stored tension can be dangerous.

Do you repair garage door openers and motors?

Yes. ProLift can diagnose opener motors, drive systems, logic boards, sensors, wiring, remotes, keypads, and smart-control issues.

Can a damaged panel be replaced instead of replacing the whole door?

Often, yes, depending on the door model, color, age, and extent of damage. We can explain when a panel repair is practical and when a full replacement is safer or more economical.

Do you service all garage door brands?

We work with LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, Craftsman, Ryobi, Overhead Door, Stanley, Wayne Dalton, and other common residential systems.
