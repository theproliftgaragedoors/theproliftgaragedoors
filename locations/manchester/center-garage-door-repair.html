Weather & your garage door

Why Manchester winters matter

Manchester sees cold, snowy winters — January lows commonly drop into the low-to-mid 20s°F — followed by humid summers in the 80s°F. Steel springs and tracks contract in the cold, losing tension right when you need the door to open reliably.

In Center specifically, older detached garages behind multi-family homes often sit unheated and get less winter attention than an attached garage would, which means iced tracks and stiff springs show up here earlier in the season than in newer parts of town.
